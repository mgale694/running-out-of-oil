# What the arithmetic can tell a reader about the real world

Source review dated 18 November 2026. Observations, models and policy options have different evidential weight.

| Question | Evidence | Interpretation and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why is oil finite while reserves change? | Oil formed over geological time. EIA explains how booked reserves reflect known, recoverable projects. [Geology](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/oil-and-petroleum-products/) · [Reserve definitions](https://www.eia.gov/Tools/faqs/faq.php?id=38&t=6) | Higher prices, better recovery, discoveries and revisions can change the available commercial stock during a human lifetime. The physical resource is drawn down with extraction. |
| How can supply decline with oil still underground? | IEA found 5.6% average observed annual post-peak decline for conventional oil fields. Its counterfactual without further capital investment estimates an average 8% annual fall in world oil production over the following decade. [IEA field study, September 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/the-implications-of-oil-and-gas-field-decline-rates/executive-summary) | Different populations and investment assumptions. An operator can invest to slow field decline or add supply elsewhere. Neither percentage can be applied unconditionally to world output. |
| What can we say about timing? | IEA WEO 2025 STEPS reaches a demand peak near 102 million barrels/day around 2030. Its Current Policies Scenario reaches 113 million barrels/day in 2050. [STEPS](https://www.iea.org/reports/world-energy-outlook-2025/stated-policies-scenario) · [CPS](https://www.iea.org/reports/world-energy-outlook-2025/current-policies-scenario) | Dated conditional scenarios. Compare policy assumptions, adoption and end use. A demand peak says nothing directly about physical exhaustion. These are different milestones, not endpoints of a probability interval. |
| Can oil remain in the ground? | OPEC WOO 2026 projects demand reaching 124 million barrels/day in 2050. IPCC AR6 examines climate pathways under which reserves remain unburned. [OPEC, June 2026](https://opec.org/pr-detail/607-18-june-2026.html) · [IPCC AR6 WGIII, chapter 6](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGIII_FullReport.pdf) | OPEC represents producers; the IPCC assesses scientific literature and conditional climate pathways. Compare assumptions and liquids definitions before calculating differences across organisations. |
| What can reduce dependence? | IEA estimates EVs displaced around 1.7 million barrels/day of oil in 2025 compared with alternative vehicle use. [Global EV Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/outlook-for-electric-mobility-chap-9-11) | Avoided demand is counterfactual. Total oil use could still grow if other uses expand. Avoid equating EV sales with replacement of the whole vehicle stock. |
| Where does oil still matter? | Industrial feedstock pathways include crude-derived and gas-derived inputs. Switching feedstock can require suitable plant design and local product availability. [IEA petrochemicals study](https://www.iea.org/reports/the-future-of-petrochemicals) · [Industrial responses to shocks](https://www.iea.org/reports/sheltering-from-oil-shocks/oil-use-in-industry) | Start with the function buyers need. Candidate adaptations include electrification, using less material and recovering or replacing a specific feedstock. Grade, cost at scale and qualification determine whether a substitute preserves access. |
| What happened during a real fuel squeeze? | In 2022 Sri Lanka's foreign-exchange shortage restricted fuel procurement. From August, its National Fuel Pass allocated weekly vehicle-category quotas. Some exporters could register for guaranteed quotas paid for in US dollars. [Central Bank annual report, ch. 3, pp. 100–101](https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/sites/default/files/cbslweb_documents/publications/annual_report/2022/en/7_Chapter_03.pdf) | A financing and import constraint caused local scarcity. The country protected access and foreign-currency earnings simultaneously. This episode cannot establish an order of priority for global geological decline. |
| What could Britain prioritise? | The April 2024 Great Britain emergency plan lists designated filling stations for critical vehicles and priority diesel distribution for key supply chains including food and health. [DESNZ response tools](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/energy-emergency-plans-priority-fuel-allocation/summary-of-response-tools-in-the-national-emergency-plan-for-fuel) | A contingency plan states what could be activated during severe disruption. Protecting a hospital may entail supplying freight, utilities and staff transport as well as the facility itself. |

## Evidence behind the demand-first comparison

In its 2020 investment review the IEA described a fall in transport fuel demand and oil prices alongside pandemic restrictions on workers, equipment and construction. Shell later reported delaying the decision on its Whale development in the US Gulf of Mexico by a year to preserve cash. It approved the project in July 2021 and announced first production in January 2025. This is one observed decision and delivery interval. The episode does not isolate demand as the cause or calibrate the model’s commissioning assumptions. [IEA, World Energy Investment 2020](https://www.iea.org/reports/world-energy-investment-2020/fuel-supply) · [Shell, July 2021 decision (release 30)](https://www.shell.com/news-and-insights/newsroom/news-and-media-releases/_jcr_content/root/main/section/simple_1285915735/call_to_action_copy__162139957/links/item0.stream/1742566965484/55dda471d98bce808e2b5d62df100a503d9b1ca8/2021-press-releases.pdf) · [Shell, January 2025 start-up and delay](https://www.shell.com/news-and-insights/newsroom/news-and-media-releases/2025/shell-starts-production-at-whale-in-the-us-gulf-of-mexico.html)

The European ACORN-BP consortium reported producing prototype PVC-free blood packs with multilayer polyolefin film. It cited €8 per pack for the existing PVC product and expected life-cycle cost parity. The report supplies neither a contracted replacement price nor a production volume available to a blood service. Naming a polymer family also leaves the origin of its feedstock unresolved. [European Commission, CORDIS period 4 report](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/958837/reporting)

## Questions that remain open

Which investments are sanctioned and delivered while demand changes? Can carriers obtain the required fuel at the delivery point and finance the imports? Which substitutes can buyers procure at scale under the same quality specification? These are measurable questions for further work. A long-run price path, a calendar date of exhaustion, and an investment return would require additional evidence and assumptions.
